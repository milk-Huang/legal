常见问题

需要注册或登录吗？

不需要。所有功能不登录就能用。登录（Sign in with Apple）是可选的，只用于将来的同步，当前版本不登录不影响任何事。

收费吗？

当前版本的全部功能免费开放，没有内购，也没有订阅。

我的数据存在哪里？

全部存在你自己的设备上，不上传。收藏、文件夹、摘录、阅读进度、历史、快照都在本机数据库里。删除 App 即删除全部数据，我们这边没有副本，也没法帮你恢复。

想留个备份，用「我的 → 导出」，支持 OPML、JSON、HTML、Markdown 四种格式。

从 Safari 分享给 LinkReader，收藏没进来？

分享扩展依赖 App Group 签名在本机把链接交给主 App，签名配置异常时这条链路会断。先试试在 LinkReader 里直接收藏 —— 主 App 内的收藏不受影响。如果只有分享进来的丢，重新安装 App 通常能修好。

打开浏览器标签，我正听的音乐断了？

已知问题。打开浏览器标签会激活音频会话，可能打断其他 App 的播放。会修。

收藏多了翻不过来？

端上的全文检索和 Spotlight 索引还没做，目前只能靠文件夹、兴趣分区和来源筛选来找。这是接下来的重点之一。

没有网络时收藏还能看吗？

能。收藏时会保存一份本地快照，断网后也能打开阅读；如果当时没能取到正文，会退回纯文本。

隐私

App 不收集任何数据，也不运营服务器。完整说明见隐私政策。

LinkReader Support

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Something broken, something you'd like to see, or just want to know whether a behaviour is expected? Start here.

Reporting a problem

The app has no account system and no in-app feedback form, so everything goes through one public issue tracker:

github.com/milk-Huang/legal/issues

Include these and we can skip a round trip:

  • iOS version and device model (Settings → General → About)
  • Steps to reproduce — where it first goes wrong
  • The link itself, if the problem is site-specific
  • A screenshot or screen recording

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Every feature works without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional and exists only for future sync — skipping it costs you nothing in this version.

Does it cost anything?

Everything in the current version is free. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own device, and never uploaded: bookmarks, folders, highlights, reading progress, history, and snapshots all live in a local database. Deleting the app deletes all of it — we hold no copy and cannot restore it for you.

For a backup, use Profile → Export. OPML, JSON, HTML, and Markdown are all supported.

I shared a link from Safari but nothing arrived

The Share Extension hands links to the main app through a local App Group container, and that path breaks when the signing configuration is off. Try saving from inside LinkReader instead — in-app saving is unaffected. If only shared links go missing, reinstalling the app usually clears it up.

Opening a browser tab stops my music

A known issue: opening a browser tab activates the audio session, which can interrupt playback from other apps. It's on the list.

Too many bookmarks to find anything?

On-device full-text search and Spotlight indexing aren't built yet, so folders, interest sections, and source filters are what you have for now. It's one of the next major pieces of work.

Does saved content work offline?

Yes. A local snapshot is saved when you bookmark, so it opens without a network connection. If the article body couldn't be fetched at the time, it falls back to plain text.

Privacy

The app collects no data and runs no servers. The full explanation is in the Privacy Policy.
